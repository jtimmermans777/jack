I'm a senior at Boston College studying Math and Computer Science. I'm interested in studying how structural model choices, especially loss functions and optimization methods, influence target performance and practical application. This includes how model design informs interpretability, reliability, and real-world implications of model predictions. I'm currently working with [Professor Finocchiaro](https://www.jessiefin.com/) in the Computer Science Department at Boston College to investigate target loss guarantees from surrogate losses. We are studying the setting of restricted hypothesis spaces, such as linear models, and how reducing the precision of model weights via quantization impacts these guarantees.
